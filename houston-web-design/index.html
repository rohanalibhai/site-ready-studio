Who this is for

This page is for owners of Houston businesses who want a website that works on a phone and is set up to be found when someone nearby searches. That includes restaurants and cafes, salons and barbers, trade businesses and small studios.

Whether you are in the Heights, Montrose, Midtown, Katy or Sugar Land, I start with what you do, your hours, your address and your photos.

I work remotely, by form, email or a quick call. I never log in to your accounts, and you keep your domain, listings and social accounts.

How the site is set up for local search

Local search depends on clear basics. This is what I set up on every Houston site.

  • Real names in real text. Your neighborhood and city, such as Houston or the Heights, are written in the page text.
  • Business details in a readable form. Your name, address, phone number and hours are described in a structured form that search engines read.
  • A Google Business Profile checklist. A short list to keep your profile matching the site: name, address, phone, hours and categories.
  • Fast phone pages. Small images, few scripts and clean code.
  • The words Houston customers type. Service pages use phrases people search for, such as "tacos near me in the Heights" or "AC repair Katy".
  • Nobody can promise you a ranking, including me. Search results change, and no one controls them. What I can do is make your site clear, fast and easy to read.
