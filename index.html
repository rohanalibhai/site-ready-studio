Questions

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. You send words and photos. I handle the rest and walk you through the two things only you can do: buying your domain and choosing hosting, both in your own name.

How do I pay?

By card through Stripe, a secure payment company. Half to start, half when you approve the finished site. The care plan is billed monthly and you can cancel any time.

What if I already have a site?

I can rebuild it, or just fix what's broken. Send me the address and I'll tell you honestly which makes sense.

Can I update it myself later?

Yes. The files are yours. Most owners prefer the care plan so they can simply text a change, but it's your choice.
