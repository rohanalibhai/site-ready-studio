All questions and answers
Do I need to know anything technical?

No. You send me words and photos, and I handle the rest. The only things I walk you through are buying your domain name in your own name and, if you skip the care plan, choosing where to host the finished files in your own account. I explain each step in plain language. If you can fill in a form or send an email, you can work with me.

What if I already have a website?

Send me the address and I will look at it. I can rebuild it, or just fix what is broken, such as titles, descriptions and text stored as images. I will tell you honestly which makes more sense, even when the answer is the smaller job. If a simple page builder would serve you better, I will say so.

How do I pay?

Through Fiverr. Message me there and I will send a custom offer for the package you choose. Payment is made through Fiverr, which holds it until the work is delivered and accepted. Nothing is due for the mock-up. A care plan, if you want one, is set up as a monthly custom offer on Fiverr and you can cancel any time. I never ask for card details by email or message.

How long does it take?

A Launch site is usually ready for your review about a week after I have your photos and details. Growth usually takes about two weeks for the first draft, and Catalog two to three. Those are first drafts, so launch depends on how quickly you review and send changes. A rush option gets a first draft in three business days, for an extra fee.

Do you use AI?

Yes, and I say so plainly. I use AI tools to draft layouts and copy quickly, which is how one person can build fast at a fair price. Every page is reviewed and edited by me before you see it. AI never invents your reviews, facts, prices or awards. Those details come from you.
