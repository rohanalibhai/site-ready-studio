Sample work

I'm starting out, so these are design concepts rather than client projects. Each one is a working demo site for a fictional business. I'd rather show honest samples than borrowed logos. Ask, and I'll make a free mock-up for your business before you pay anything.

Concept, not a client

Restaurant concept

Bayou & Bell Kitchen

A one-page site for a fictional Gulf Coast and Southern kitchen in Houston Heights, built so a hungry customer on a phone can read the menu, check today's hours and call or get directions in one tap.

  • Full menu written as real text with prices and dietary markers, not a photo or PDF
  • Shows whether the kitchen is open today and highlights today in the weekly hours
  • Call and Directions buttons stay pinned at the top of every screen
  • Map, parking note and a private events and catering inquiry that opens an email

Concept, not a client

Clothing boutique concept

Oleander Row

A phone-first catalog site for a fictional independent clothing boutique in Houston Heights, with new arrivals first, real prices and sizes, a size guide and buy buttons that link to the owner's own checkout.

  • New arrivals lead the page, each with price, sizes left and colors as real text
  • Buy buttons are ready to link to the owner's own Shopify, Square or Stripe checkout
  • A size guide table plus shipping, returns and exchanges in plain words
  • Hours, a neighborhood map, Directions and one-tap call for shoppers on the way over

Concept, not a client

Small-batch skincare brand concept

Heronfield Skin

A one-page storefront for a fictional Houston skincare maker, with six products, full ingredient lists, a morning and evening routine guide, and buy buttons that would link to the brand's own checkout.

  • Six products with size, price, skin types and key ingredients, each with its own buy button
  • Full ingredient lists as real text, plus a plain-English glossary
  • Morning and evening routine guide that tells customers what to use and in what order
  • Careful wording: cosmetic benefits only, a patch-test note, and clear shipping and returns
