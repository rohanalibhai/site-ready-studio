Getting started

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. You send me words and photos, and I handle the rest. The only things I walk you through are buying your domain name in your own name and, if you skip the care plan, choosing where to host the finished files in your own account. I explain each step in plain language. If you can fill in a form or send an email, you can work with me.

What is the free mock-up, and is there a catch?

It is a real draft of your site, built from the details you give me, which I send before you pay anything. There is no catch. You look at it, and if it is not right for you, you owe nothing. I am starting out, so a mock-up is how I earn your trust.

What do you need from me to start?

A short form or a quick call covering what you do, your hours and address, and what you want visitors to do. Send photos you own, any wording you already like, and the address of your current site if you have one. For menus, prices or service lists, send them as text. I fill in nothing you have not told me.

What if I already have a website?

Send me the address and I will look at it. I can rebuild it, or just fix what is broken, such as titles, descriptions and text stored as images. I will tell you honestly which makes more sense, even when the answer is the smaller job. If a simple page builder would serve you better, I will say so.

Do you work with businesses outside Houston?

Yes. I am based in Houston, Texas, and I work with small businesses anywhere in the US, remotely, by email and, if you like, a call. Everything happens online: the mock-up, changes, approval and launch. For local search, I write the site around your own city and service area, whatever it is, using details you give me.

Pricing and payment

How do I pay?

Through Fiverr. Message me there and I will send a custom offer for the package you choose. Payment is made through Fiverr, which holds it until the work is delivered and accepted. Nothing is due for the mock-up. A care plan, if you want one, is set up as a monthly custom offer on Fiverr and you can cancel any time. I never ask for card details by email or message.

How much does a website cost?

There are three website packages, each a one-time price: Launch for simple sites of one to five pages, Growth for menus, service lists and forms, and Catalog for product businesses. Launch has a launch price, and Growth and Catalog start from a stated amount. For Growth and Catalog you get a written quote before any work starts. The pricing page shows current figures.

What if I do not like the mock-up or the site?

If the mock-up is not right, you owe nothing. Once we begin, your package includes rounds of changes, two for Launch and three for Growth and Catalog, so we can reshape the site until it fits. If you are unhappy at any point, tell me and we talk it through before anything else happens.

What is not included?

The domain name, which costs about $12 a year and is bought in your own name. Also not included: custom shopping carts, custom software, paid ads, photo or video shoots, and posting on your social accounts. I also do not log in to your accounts. If you need one of these, I will say so before work starts and point you to the right option.

The build and timeline

How long does it take?

A Launch site is usually ready for your review about a week after I have your photos and details. Growth usually takes about two weeks for the first draft, and Catalog two to three. Those are first drafts, so launch depends on how quickly you review and send changes. A rush option gets a first draft in three business days, for an extra fee.

How many rounds of changes do I get?

Launch includes two rounds of changes, and Growth and Catalog include three. A round is a batch of notes sent together, such as wording, photos or layout. If you still need more after that, we agree the next step together before any extra work. Extra pages are a flat $75 each, so you always know the cost.

Which package do I need?

Launch suits a simple presence: one to five pages with hours, a map and contact. Growth adds full menus, service lists, galleries and quote or booking-request forms. Catalog adds a product catalog of up to 24 products with buy buttons. Tell me about your business and I will recommend the smallest package that does the job, even if that is the cheapest.

AI and quality

Do you use AI?

Yes, and I say so plainly. I use AI tools to draft layouts and copy quickly, which is how one person can build fast at a fair price. Every page is reviewed and edited by me before you see it. AI never invents your reviews, facts, prices or awards. Those details come from you.

Who checks the work, and what do you check?

I do. I review every page myself, then test the site on a phone and a computer before it goes live. I check that links, buttons and forms work, that your details are correct, and that nothing claims more than you told me. You then review it and sign off. Nothing is published until you approve it.

Being found on Google

Will I show up on Google?

I set your site up so it can be found: real text instead of text in images, clear page titles and descriptions, business details markup, fast phone pages and wording your customers actually use. No one can promise a ranking, and I will not. What I can do is make sure nothing on your site gets in the way of being found.

Do you set up my Google Business Profile?

I do not log in to your accounts, so I do not set it up for you. As an add-on, I prepare the wording, categories and a photo checklist, then walk you through it. For Growth and Catalog sites, a checklist is included. Keeping your name, address and hours the same on the site and the profile helps people trust it.

Social content

Do you post for me?

No. I write the captions, plan the calendar and describe the visuals, then you approve it and post from your own account. Your accounts stay yours, and I never ask for a login, never schedule posts and never reply to comments. That keeps you in control of what goes out under your name, and means no one can lock you out.

What do the monthly content plans include?

Three tiers. Starter is one platform with 12 captions, visual notes, a calendar and a summary. Growth is two platforms with 16 captions, 4 short-video scripts and a mid-month calendar tweak. Full content is three platforms with 24 captions, 8 scripts, a weekly calendar and a monthly call. Not included: ads, shoots, posting or replies. Pricing is introductory for my first three clients.

Can I order through Fiverr?

Yes, every order is placed and paid through Fiverr. Websites start from my website gig, and social content from my restaurant captions gig, where there is also a one-week content pack for restaurants, from $40, if you want to try a small piece of work first. You can also just ask me a question through the quote form.

After launch and ownership

Who owns the site, the domain and the files?

You do. The domain is always bought and kept in your own name, and I never hold it. Without a care plan, you host the finished files wherever you like, in your own account. With Care or Care Plus, I host the site for you as part of the plan, and if you cancel you get the finished files to host anywhere. There is no lock-in contract. I would rather earn the next month than trap you.

What does the care plan cover, and what if I cancel?

Care is $49 a month and includes hosting, text and photo updates on request, hours and holiday changes, a monthly check that everything works and help with your domain. Care Plus is $99 and adds a new section or page each month, a seasonal refresh and a monthly search check-up. You can cancel any time. If you do, you get the finished files to host anywhere, and your domain stays in your own name.

Can I sell products on my site?

Yes, with the Catalog package. It shows up to 24 products with photos, prices and options, plus buy buttons that link to your own checkout in Shopify, Square or Etsy. It is not a custom shopping cart, so payments and customer details stay in a trusted tool that belongs to you.

Can I update the site myself later?

Yes. Without a care plan, the finished files are yours to host and edit, or to hand to anyone you hire. Most owners prefer the care plan, so they can text a change or a new photo and have it done. Either way, I give you a plain guide at handover showing what lives where. It is your choice, and you can switch at any time.
