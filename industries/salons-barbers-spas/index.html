Where these sites usually fall short

Owners of hair salons, barbershops, nail studios and spas who want clients to see services and prices and ask to book without a phone call.

1

No price list

When a site hides prices, many people assume it is expensive and move on. A clear menu of services and prices, as text, sets expectations and saves calls.

2

Booking is a maze

If the only option is a phone number that rings through, evening and weekend visitors drop off. A simple request form or a link to your booking tool gives them a next step.

3

Your work is not shown

A salon is judged by its results. Photos of your own work, with short captions, help new clients picture themselves in your chair.

Questions from salons and barbers

Can clients book an appointment on the site?

They can send a booking request through a form, which arrives in your email with the service and times they prefer. If you already use a booking tool, I add a button that opens it. The form is optional, and without it the button opens your booking tool. I do not build a custom live calendar, so the tool you choose handles the actual scheduling.

Do I have to show my prices?

No, it is your choice, but I recommend it. A price list as plain text answers the first question most new clients have and cuts down calls. If your pricing varies, you can show starting prices and say that the final cost depends on the consultation.

Can the site show my team separately?

Yes. Each stylist, barber or therapist can have a short profile with their specialties and a link to request them. It is optional and priced in your quote. I use only the details and photos each person gives me, with their permission.
