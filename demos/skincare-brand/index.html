How to use it

Your routine, in order

Thinnest to thickest, morning and night. You do not need all six. Skip any step your skin is happy without.

Morning

About three minutes

  1. Rinse or cleanseSplash with lukewarm water. If your skin feels oily, use a small pump of Soft Cloud Cleansing Milk instead.
  2. Rainwater EssenceShake a few drops into your palms and press onto damp skin.
  3. Still Water SerumTwo or three drops while skin is still a little damp.
  4. Porch Light MoisturizerA pea-sized amount, warmed between your fingertips.
  5. Your sunscreenWe do not make a sunscreen. Finish with one you like, every day.

Evening

About five minutes

  1. Soft Cloud Cleansing MilkMassage onto dry skin for about a minute, then rinse with a warm cloth.
  2. Rainwater EssencePat in with your hands. No cotton pad needed.
  3. Still Water SerumTwo or three drops over face and neck.
  4. Night Garden Face Oil or Porch LightOil if your skin feels dry or tight, cream if it feels normal. Some nights, both: cream first, then oil.
  5. Pocket BalmA thin layer on lips and any dry patches before bed.

New to a product? Add one at a time, a week apart, so you can tell what your skin likes. Patch test first; stop if irritation occurs.

The words, in plain English

Glycerin
Draws water into the top layer of skin so it feels softer. One of the most common ingredients in skincare.
Sodium hyaluronate
A form of hyaluronic acid. Holds a lot of water, which helps skin look smooth and plump.
Niacinamide
A form of vitamin B3. Used to help skin look more even and feel less dry.
Panthenol
Pro-vitamin B5. A softening ingredient that helps skin feel comfortable.
Squalane
A light, silky oil that is similar to oils skin makes itself. Softens without feeling heavy.
Oat kernel extract
From the oat grain. A gentle ingredient that helps skin feel calm after cleansing.

Questions

Good to know

How long does each product last?

Unopened, about 18 months from the batch date on the base. Once opened, use within 6 months (the small open-jar symbol on the back says the same). Keep them out of hot cars and direct sun.

How do I patch test?

Put a small amount on your inner forearm or behind your ear once a day for two days. If you see redness, itching or stinging, do not use it on your face. Patch test first; stop if irritation occurs.

Is anything scented?

We add no fragrance or essential oils to any product. Some plant oils have a faint natural smell of their own, which can vary a little from batch to batch.

Can these help with a skin condition?

No. These are cosmetic products for everyday care. If you have a skin condition or a reaction that worries you, please see a doctor or a dermatologist.

What if a product does not suit me?

Write to us within 30 days of delivery and tell us what happened. Unopened items can be sent back for a full refund. For opened items we will talk it through with you and make it right.
