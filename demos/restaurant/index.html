About the kitchen

Named for the bayou out back and the bell by the pass.

Bayou & Bell is a small neighborhood kitchen cooking the food of the Gulf Coast: Creole and Cajun staples, Texas barbecue habits and Southern Sunday-dinner sides. When a plate is ready, the cook rings the brass bell, and that sound is most of what our kitchen is about.

We keep the menu short so we can make it properly. Stocks, roux, pickles and dressings are made in house. Seafood comes from Gulf suppliers when it is in season, and the menu changes when it is not.
