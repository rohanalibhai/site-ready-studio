How to measure

  1. BustAround the fullest part, tape level across your back.
  2. WaistAround the narrowest part, usually just above the belly button.
  3. HipAround the fullest part, feet together.

Not sure? Call or come in. Exchanges for a different size are free in store.

The shop

Chosen by the owner, one rail at a time

Oleander Row is a small, owner-run shop. The owner is also the buyer, so every piece on the floor was tried on, washed and worn before it was ordered.

  • 01
    Small runs

    Six to twelve of a style, then it moves on. You will not see your outfit on everyone at brunch.

  • 02
    Made for this heat

    Linen, cotton and breathable blends first. Wool only for the few weeks that need it.

  • 03
    Fit help in person

    Bring a piece you love and we will find what goes with it, no pressure to buy.

The fine print, made short

Shipping, returns & exchanges

  • Shipping

    Flat $8 anywhere in the US, free on orders over $150. Orders leave the shop within two business days.

  • Local pickup

    Free. Choose pickup at checkout and collect from the counter the next day we are open.

  • Returns

    Within 14 days, unworn with tags on, for a refund to your original payment. Sale pieces are final.

  • Exchanges

    Free in store for a different size or color within 30 days. By mail, we cover the return label.
