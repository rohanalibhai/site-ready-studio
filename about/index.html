Who you're working with

I'm Rohan Alibhai, and I run Site Ready Studio on my own from Houston, Texas. There is no sales team and no hand-off to a junior. You deal with one person from the first message to launch day.

I started the studio after noticing how many good local businesses have a web address that doesn't load, or a page nobody has touched in years. The food is good, the shop is lovely, the work is solid, and the website makes the place look closed. That seemed fixable.

I'm starting out, and I would rather say that than pretend otherwise. That is why I make a free mock-up before you pay anything, and why my sample work is labeled as concept work rather than borrowed logos.

What the studio does

I build clear, fast websites for small businesses anywhere in the US, remotely, and I look after them afterwards if you want me to.

What the websites are

Simple, phone-first sites that show what you offer, your hours, a map and one-tap contact. Real text, not pictures of text.

Packages for different needs

Launch for a few pages, Growth for menus, service lists and forms, and Catalog for businesses that sell products.

Care after launch

With a care plan, I host the site, keep it updated and check it each month, so it doesn't go stale the way so many do.

Social content, if you want it

Captions, calendars and scripts written for you to approve and post from your own accounts.

How a site gets built

Six steps, and no meetings you don't need.

  1. One

    You tell me about the business

    A short form or a quick call: what you do, your hours, your photos and any site you like the look of.

  2. Two

    AI drafts the structure and copy

    From your details, AI tools produce a first layout and wording. They work from what you gave me, not from guesses.

  3. Three

    I edit and design

    I rewrite the draft in plain language, fix anything that sounds off, and shape the design to suit your business.

  4. Four

    I check it on phone and computer

    I test every page, link, button and form on a small screen and a large one before you see it.

  5. Five

    You review it

    You get a free mock-up first. Then you send changes in rounds until it is right, and approve the final site.

  6. Six

    It goes live

    I walk you through pointing your own domain at the new site and test it on phones and computers. You host the finished files yourself, or I host them on a care plan. Either way you get a plain guide to what lives where.

Where AI helps and where it doesn't

I use a small set of AI assistants, and each has one job: one helps with research, one with drafting, one with building and one with checking. Splitting the work this way keeps each step simple and easy to review.

I review every page before a client sees it. AI never invents reviews, facts, prices or awards. Your details come from you, and if something is missing from the page, I ask you rather than make it up.

AI is good at fast first drafts. It is not good at knowing your customers, your neighborhood or what you are proud of. That part comes from you, and from the editing I do afterwards.

How your customers find you

Say someone nearby types "clothing store near me" or "plumber in Katy". Here is what I set up so your site is ready to be found.

  • Real text. Your menu, services and prices are written as text, because search engines can read text and cannot read a photo of it.
  • Clear titles and descriptions. Each page gets its own plain title and description, so people and search engines know what it is about.
  • Business details markup. Hidden labels in the page that tell search engines your name, address, hours and what you offer.
  • Matching details everywhere. A checklist to keep your Google Business Profile the same as the site: name, address, phone and hours.
  • Fast pages on a phone. Images are sized sensibly and pages are kept light, because most customers search on a phone.
  • Wording your customers use. I write with the terms people in your trade type, such as "cut and color" or "AC repair".
  • An honest limit. Nobody can promise a ranking, and I won't. I can make sure nothing on your site stands in the way.

How I find the businesses I write to

If you have heard from me first, here is how that happened. I look at public listings for local businesses, and I check whether each site loads on a phone. I use only public information.

When I see something I could help with, I write to the business's public address and say what I found. I keep it short, and I don't claim to know you. If I hear nothing, I may follow up once, and that is all.

If you reply "no thanks", that ends it. I won't write again.

Why it's fast and fairly priced

A studio with an office, a team and a sales department has to charge for all of it. I don't have those costs. I work alone, and I use AI for the slow part, the first draft, so my time goes where it matters: editing, design and checking.

That is how one person can deliver a first draft in about a week at a flat price you see up front. For larger jobs you get a written quote before any work starts.

What I won't do

  • Invent reviews. If you have real ones you can share, I will lay them out well. If not, that section stays empty.
  • Promise search rankings. No honest person can. I set the groundwork and tell you what I did.
  • Lock you into a contract. Your domain is bought in your name. Care plans can be canceled any time, and then you get the finished files to host anywhere.
  • Hold your domain hostage. It is always bought in your name, and it stays in your name.
  • Sell you more than you need. If a simple page builder would serve you better than hiring me, I will tell you.

What happens after launch

Care plan

$49 a month, cancel any time: I host the site, and you get text and photo updates on request, hours and holiday changes, and a monthly check that everything works. If you cancel, you get the finished files to host anywhere.

Care Plus

$99 a month: everything in Care, plus a new section or page each month, a seasonal refresh and a monthly search check-up, with requests handled within one business day.

Social content

If you want it, I write the month's captions and calendar. You approve them and post from your own accounts. I never ask for a login.

Or go it alone

Without a care plan, you host the finished files wherever you like, in your own account. You can edit them yourself or hire someone else, and I will hand over a plain guide to help.
