Content that fills the room, not just the feed

A kitchen has plenty to show, but the owner has no time to write about it. I turn the menu, the people and the week's specials into posts you can approve in minutes.

I would start with Instagram, because food and drink are visual and local diners search and save there. Facebook is the usual second platform for events and older regulars, and a short-video platform third.

What the posts are about

Menu spotlights

One dish or drink at a time, with the detail that makes someone hungry.

Behind the kitchen

Prep, the pass, the first pour. Real routine, shown plainly.

People and regulars

Staff introductions with their consent, and the neighborhood around you.

Hours and specials

Happy hour, brunch, holiday hours and last-call reminders that people actually save.
